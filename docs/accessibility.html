Accessibility Statement

This app exists for people who find ordinary volume too loud. A website about it should not be hard to use. Here is exactly where this one stands, including the parts that are not finished.

Applies to granularvolume.com and to the privacy and terms pages at rzuss.github.io/granular-volume-privacy · Published September 7, 2026 · Last reviewed September 14, 2026

Quiet Dial is made by one developer. That is not an excuse for an inaccessible site, but it does shape how this statement is written: everything below was tested rather than assumed, and where something has not been tested it says so.

The standard we aim at

This website aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA, the conformance level referenced by Israeli Standard 5568 and by the European Accessibility Act.

Conformance status: partially conformant. Partially conformant means most of the standard is met and some parts are not yet. We are not claiming full conformance, because that claim has not been earned by a formal audit.

What has been done, and verified

Checked on September 7, 2026, page by page, across all pages of this site:

  • Every image carries a text alternative.
  • Body text sits at a contrast ratio of about 17.8:1 against its background, and secondary text at about 7.7:1. Both exceed the AA requirement of 4.5:1, and both meet AAA.
  • Re-measured on September 12, 2026: the label on the main call-to-action button reaches 6.3:1 at one end of its gradient and 5.4:1 at the other. An earlier version of that button sat below the AA threshold for its text size; the colour was darkened and the shortcoming is gone, not merely disclosed.
  • Every page declares its language, including the German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese pages, so a screen reader pronounces them correctly.
  • Headings run in order, with a single first-level heading per page, so a screen reader can be used to navigate by structure.
  • Every page has a "skip to the main content" link as its first keyboard stop, in the language of that page.
  • Every page has a main landmark, so assistive technology can jump straight to the content.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible, as a high-contrast outline around whatever is focused.
  • The site is plain HTML and CSS. It works without JavaScript, it reflows to any window size, and text can be enlarged with the browser's own zoom without content being lost.
  • Measured on September 12, 2026, at a 320 pixel viewport: the page reflows with no sideways scrolling, text at double size loses nothing, and the spacing overrides the standard asks for (line height, letter, word and paragraph spacing) clip no text anywhere. Those are the reflow, resize-text and text-spacing requirements, tested rather than assumed.
  • Nothing on the site moves, flashes, plays automatically or times out.
  • Added on September 14, 2026: an accessibility button on every page of granularvolume.com, at the bottom left. It opens a small panel that can enlarge the page content, switch to high contrast, underline every link and change to a plainer font, and it links to this statement. It is part of this site, not a third-party plugin: it loads nothing from anyone else, sets no cookie, and remembers your choices only in the open browser tab, until you close it. Without JavaScript it is a plain link to this statement. It is an aid, and it does not replace the site itself meeting the standard, which is what the rest of this page is about.

Browsers and assistive technology

The site is plain HTML and CSS with no framework, so it depends on nothing exotic. It is expected to work in the current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, on desktop and on Android and iOS, and it respects the operating system's reduced-motion setting.

  • Tested on September 7, 2026: keyboard-only navigation in Chrome on Windows, including the skip link, focus order and focus visibility, on every page.
  • Tested on September 12, 2026: reflow at a 320 pixel viewport, text at 200%, and the standard's text-spacing overrides, on the home page. The accessibility tree every screen reader reads from was inspected on the same day: thirty keyboard stops, tab order matching the reading order, no control without a name, and no two links sharing a name while leading to different pages.
  • Not yet tested: a full pass with NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack. The structure those tools rely on is in place and has been inspected, but that is not the same as having run them.

Known limitations, stated plainly

These are the parts we know are not finished. If one of them stands between you and something you need, write to us and we will send you that content in a form that works for you.

  • No formal audit. The checks above were done by the developer, not by an independent accessibility auditor, and not with a full assistive-technology test matrix.
  • Screen readers have not been tested end to end. The structural work that screen readers rely on is in place, but no full pass has been done with NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver.
  • Videos on the site are embedded from YouTube and are used as illustration. Captions on them are provided by YouTube rather than authored by us, and no audio description track exists. Nothing on this site depends on a video to be understood.
  • Pages in German, Spanish and French received the same structural work as the English pages, but their text has not been reviewed by a native speaker for plain-language clarity.

About the app itself

This statement covers the website. The Android app is a separate piece of software, and honesty is worth more here than a reassuring sentence:

  • The app's on-screen controls carry spoken labels for TalkBack, including the close button, the information button, the mute button, the louder control and each volume step.
  • From version 1.5.0 the app also speaks the level itself. Changing the volume announces the new value, so the app's main action no longer depends on seeing the screen, and the two text links on the upgrade sheet are announced as buttons rather than as plain text.
  • The app has not been formally audited for accessibility, and it has not been fully tested with TalkBack in real use. We are not claiming it is accessible.
  • If you use a screen reader or a switch device and the app does not work for you, please tell us. That is a bug report we want, and it will be treated as one.

Found something that does not work?

Tell us and we will fix it, or get you the content another way in the meantime. Say what page you were on, what you were trying to do, and what happened. If you can, mention the browser and any assistive technology you use, because it makes the problem far quicker to reproduce.

  • Email: rotemzus@gmail.com
  • Responsible person: Rotem Zussman, developer and accessibility contact for this site.
  • We will acknowledge your message within 10 business days and tell you what we intend to do about it and by when.
  • If you need content in another form while a problem is being fixed, ask, and we will send it as plain text or in whatever format suits you.

Email is the accessible channel for everything here. There is no phone queue, no form to fight with, and no account to create.

How this statement is kept honest

It was prepared on September 7, 2026 after an actual review of this site, not from a template, revised on September 12, 2026 after a second round of measurement, and on September 14, 2026 when the accessibility button was added. It is reviewed when the site changes materially and at least once a year. Every page added to this site is expected to carry the same skip link, main landmark and visible focus indicator described above.

If we ever claim full conformance here, it will be because an audit was done and can be pointed at.

מה נבדק ותוקן, נכון ל־7 בספטמבר 2026

  • לכל תמונה יש טקסט חלופי.
  • ניגודיות הטקסט הראשי היא כ־17.8:1 והמשני כ־7.7:1, מעל הנדרש ברמה AA.
  • נמדד מחדש ב־12 בספטמבר 2026: תווית כפתור הפעולה הראשי מגיעה ל־6.3:1 ול־5.4:1 בשני קצות המעבר. בגרסה קודמת הכפתור היה מתחת לסף, הצבע הוכהה והליקוי תוקן.
  • נמדד ב־12 בספטמבר 2026 ברוחב 320 פיקסל: הדף נערך מחדש ללא גלילה לרוחב, טקסט בגודל כפול אינו מאבד תוכן, ועקיפות הריווח שהתקן דורש אינן חותכות טקסט באף מקום.
  • בכל דף מוגדרת שפת הדף, כולל הדפים בגרמנית, ספרדית וצרפתית.
  • מבנה הכותרות תקין והיררכי, וניתן לנווט לפיו בקורא מסך.
  • בכל דף קיים קישור דילוג לתוכן הראשי, והוא תחנת המקלדת הראשונה.
  • בכל דף קיים אזור תוכן ראשי מסומן, וסימון פוקוס נראה לעין בניווט מקלדת.
  • האתר בנוי HTML ו־CSS בלבד, עובד ללא JavaScript, ומאפשר הגדלת טקסט בדפדפן ללא אובדן תוכן.
  • אין באתר תוכן נע, מהבהב, מתנגן אוטומטית או מוגבל בזמן.
  • נוסף ב־14 בספטמבר 2026: כפתור נגישות בכל דף באתר granularvolume.com, בפינה השמאלית התחתונה. הוא פותח חלונית שמאפשרת להגדיל את תוכן הדף, לעבור לניגודיות גבוהה, להוסיף קו תחתון לכל קישור ולעבור לגופן פשוט יותר, ומקשר להצהרה זו. הכפתור הוא חלק מהאתר ואינו תוסף של צד שלישי: הוא אינו טוען דבר מגורם אחר, אינו שומר עוגיות, וזוכר את הבחירות רק בלשונית הדפדפן הפתוחה, עד לסגירתה. ללא JavaScript הוא קישור רגיל להצהרה. הכפתור הוא עזר, ואינו מחליף את עמידת האתר עצמו בתקן.

דפדפנים וטכנולוגיה מסייעת

האתר בנוי HTML ו־CSS בלבד ואמור לעבוד בגרסאות העדכניות של Chrome, Firefox, Safari ו־Edge, במחשב ובטלפון, ומכבד את הגדרת הפחתת התנועה של מערכת ההפעלה. נבדק ב־7 בספטמבר 2026 בניווט מקלדת בלבד ב־Chrome בכל הדפים, וב־12 בספטמבר 2026 נבדקו גם עריכה מחדש ברוחב 320 פיקסל, טקסט בגודל 200%, ועץ הנגישות שקוראי המסך מסתמכים עליו. טרם נבדק במלואו עם קוראי מסך (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, TalkBack).
