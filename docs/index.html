Questions

Do I need root or a computer?
No. No root, no ADB and no computer. Install it from Google Play, open it once and allow it to display over other apps.
Does it work with Bluetooth headphones and speakers?
The quiet steps apply over Bluetooth as well as on the speaker. On a small number of phone and headphone combinations they have no effect over Bluetooth, although they work on the speaker. The seven days with everything open are the time to check yours.
Does it lower phone calls?
Calls in apps such as WhatsApp, yes. Regular calls over the mobile network, no: Android carries them on a separate path that runs no audio effects, so no app can lower them below the phone's own minimum.
Does the floating dial get in the way?
Android has no setting below its lowest step, so going lower takes a control of its own on screen. You can fold the dial into a small tab at the edge of the screen: the quiet level stays, your volume buttons keep working, and a Quick Settings tile turns the control on and off.
What does it collect?
Nothing. The app has no internet permission at all, no ads, no account and no tracking. The source code is public under GPL-3.0.
Does it work on tablets?
Yes, on Android phones and tablets alike, from Android 9.
