Granular Volume

Quieter than your phone or tablet allows.

Sometimes the lowest volume setting is still too loud, on a phone or a tablet. And sometimes the volume buttons themselves stop working. Granular Volume puts the whole volume range on your screen: one floating slider with normal volume above your device's minimum line, and the quiet steps Android leaves out below it.

When the lowest setting is still too loud

The first notch above mute can be too much for a quiet room, a sleeping baby, or a sensitive pair of headphones, on a phone or a tablet. Android will not go lower. Granular Volume does, on both.

Quieter than minimum

Fine volume steps beneath the lowest hardware step, for when even the minimum is too much.

🎛️

The whole range on screen

Above the device-minimum line the slider sets normal system volume, so it stands in for worn or broken volume buttons. Even 5 dB steps on speaker and wired; on Bluetooth it follows your device's real hardware steps, so every bar is audible.

🔕

Mutes media, not alarms

One tap silences media and leaves the alarm volume where it was. The quiet steps do lower alarms too, so test your level before a morning you cannot miss.

📱

Phones and tablets alike

Tablets often have an even higher minimum volume than phones. Granular Volume works exactly the same way on both.

🎚️

A floating volume control

It floats over any app. Drag it anywhere, tuck it to an edge, close it with one tap.

🛡️

No takeover

Your buttons and the system panel keep working. The dial sits on top as an overlay and controls only the level.

⬇️

Quick Settings tile

Swipe down, tap once. Toggle the control on or off from your notification shade without opening the app.

🔒

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no account, no network access. Open source under GPL-3.0.
