Fixes a bug where volume-up presses could keep pulling the volume back down
instead of raising it. Presses now climb out of the quiet zone and carry on
raising the volume normally.

On a regular phone call the quiet steps are shown dimmed, with a short note.
Android carries call audio on a path that runs no audio effects, so nothing
can lower it further. App calls such as WhatsApp keep the full range on most
phones.

Build change for F-Droid: no more Google-encrypted dependency metadata.
