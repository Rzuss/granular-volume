Fixes a bug where volume-up presses could keep pulling the volume back down
instead of raising it. Presses now climb smoothly out of the quiet zone and
carry on raising the volume normally.

If something else keeps raising the volume while the quiet zone is holding it
down, a second raise by you now wins immediately.

On a regular phone call the quiet steps are shown dimmed, with a short note.
Android carries call audio on a path that runs no audio effects, so nothing
can lower it further. App calls such as WhatsApp keep the full range.
